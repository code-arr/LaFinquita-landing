Your ticket

Prices and payment

Prices are shown in US dollars. Depending on your card, the charge may be made in Argentine pesos at the exchange rate applied by the payment processor at the time of payment. Your bank may apply its own fees.

Cancellations and refunds

  • If the event is cancelled by La Finquita 1920, you get a full refund to the same card, or you may use the ticket for another date.
  • If the event is rescheduled, your ticket is valid for the new date. If you cannot attend, you can request a full refund within 7 days of the announcement.
  • Tickets cannot be refunded if you change your mind or cannot attend, except as stated above or as required by Argentine consumer law (Ley 24.240). You may request a date change by contacting us at least 48 hours before the event, subject to availability.
  • Refunds are processed within 10 business days. The time for the money to appear on your statement depends on your bank.

Personal data

We use your name, email, country and passport/ID only to issue and validate your ticket and to contact you about the event, in accordance with Argentine Law 25.326. We do not sell or share your data except with the payment processor.
